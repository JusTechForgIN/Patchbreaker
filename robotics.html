ROBOTICS / ELECTRONICS

Proper Handling and Use of Electronics Tools and Equipment

Demonstrating proper safety precautions while working with electronics.

01

Before Starting

Prepare the workspace and equipment safely before beginning an electronics activity.

  • Disconnect the power source before starting to work. Electrical equipment should be de-energized before work when exposure to live parts is possible. (OSHA, 1910.333)
  • Check if the tools and equipment are in good condition.
  • Prepare all the tools and equipment needed.
  • Keep the area clean, dry, and organized.
  • Wear appropriate personal protective equipment when electrical hazards are present. (OSHA, 1910.335)
  • Remove metallic jewelry on hands and avoid wearing loose clothing when there is a possibility of contacting exposed energized parts.
02

Proper Handling and Use of Tools

Use the correct equipment for each task and handle tools carefully.

Multimeter

Used to measure voltage, current, resistance, and continuity. Make sure the selected setting is correct before testing.

Soldering Station

Used to permanently connect electronic components. Hold it by the insulated handle and return it to its stand after use.

Bench DC Power Supply

Used to control or adjust current and voltage in an electric circuit and can provide current and voltage limits.

Wire Stripper

Used to remove insulation from wires without damaging the conductor.

Wire Cutter

Used to cut electrical wires cleanly. Never cut live wires.

Screwdriver

Used for different types of screws and comes in different sizes and blade types.

Tweezers

Used for holding and positioning small electronic components.

Breadboard

Used for prototyping and testing circuits without soldering.

LCR Meter

Used to measure inductance, capacitance, and resistance.

Magnifier, Lamp, and Desk Clamp

Used to see small components clearly and hold the circuit securely.

Computer

Used to simulate circuits and show circuit schematics.

Calculator

Used to solve calculations and double-check measurements and expected values.

Glue Gun

Used to attach project parts and organize wires. Handle hot glue carefully.

03

While Working

Follow safety rules throughout the activity.

  • Follow all safety rules.
  • Use the correct tools or equipment for each task.
  • Keep your hands dry when doing the task.
  • Keep food and drinks away from the work area.
  • Never touch exposed live wires. (OSHA, 1910.333)
  • Handle hot tools carefully.
  • Keep wires organized to prevent accidents.
04

When an Accident Happens

Stop work and notify the person in charge.

  • Stop working immediately and stay calm.
  • Turn off the power source if it is safe to do so.
  • Move away from the danger and notify the teacher or person in charge.
  • If a fire happens, disconnect the power source only if it is safe to do so and follow the established emergency procedure.
  • Report the accident or fire to the person in charge.
  • If a burning smell appears from wires or equipment, stop using the equipment and notify the person in charge.
  • Use the first aid kit if needed.
  • Do not continue working until the area and equipment are declared safe.
05

Work Area Organization

Keep the workspace clean and organized.

  • Keep the work area clean and organized at all times.
  • Arrange tools and materials neatly before and after use.
  • Remove unnecessary objects from the workspace.
  • Keep wires and cables organized to prevent accidents.
  • Return all tools and equipment to their proper places.
  • Dispose of waste materials properly.
  • Work in a well-ventilated area.
06

After Finishing

Make sure the workspace and equipment are safe before leaving.

  • Turn off and unplug all equipment.
  • Clean the work area.
  • Return all tools to their proper places.
  • Dispose of waste materials properly.
  • Check that all equipment is safe before leaving.
  • Make sure the work area is free from hazards before leaving.

⚠️ Safety Reminder

Always prioritize safety when working with electronics. Use the correct equipment, follow instructions, and ask the teacher or person in charge for assistance when you are unsure about a task.

References / Citations
